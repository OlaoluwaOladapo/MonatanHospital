How We Started

A Doctor Who Chose His Community

Monatan Central Specialist Hospital & IVF Center was born from a simple but powerful conviction — that world-class healthcare shouldn't be reserved for those who can travel to the big city. That people in Monatan, Ibadan and across Oyo State deserve specialist care right where they live.

Founded by Dr. Olakunle Oloko, the hospital began as a vision to close the gap between community healthcare and specialist expertise. Today, it stands as a full-service specialist hospital and IVF center, serving thousands of patients with the kind of care that goes beyond clinical expertise — the kind that sees the person, not just the condition.

From consultations to complex surgeries, from maternity care to fertility treatment, Monatan Central has become a place where people come not just to be treated, but to be restored.

Founder & Lead Specialist Physician

Dr. Olakunle Oloko is the heart behind Monatan Central Specialist Hospital & IVF Center. A highly experienced specialist physician with a passion for both clinical excellence and community service, he has dedicated his career to bringing quality healthcare closer to the people.

Known for his approachable bedside manner and deep commitment to patient welfare, Dr. Oloko doesn't just treat conditions — he takes time to understand each patient's full story. His presence on social media reflects this same philosophy: education, transparency, and removing the fear from healthcare.

With hands-on experience in consultations, surgical procedures, and fertility medicine, Dr. Oloko leads a team that shares his conviction — that every life deserves exceptional care.

"Medicine is not just science. It is the art of being present for someone in their most vulnerable moment." — Dr. Olakunle Oloko
