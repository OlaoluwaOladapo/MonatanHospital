General & Specialist Consultations

Outpatient

Our consultation service is the foundation of everything we do. Whether you are visiting for the first time or following up on an existing condition, our specialists take time to listen, examine, and explain.

  • Thorough medical history and examination
  • Accurate diagnosis and treatment planning
  • Specialist referrals where required
  • Follow-up and chronic disease management

Surgical Procedures

Inpatient / Outpatient

Our surgical team performs a wide range of procedures with precision, safety, and care. From minor operations to more complex interventions, every surgery is approached with the highest clinical standards.

  • General and elective surgeries
  • Laparoscopic (keyhole) procedures
  • Pre- and post-operative care
  • Anaesthesia and recovery support

IVF & Fertility Treatment

Specialist Centre

Our IVF and fertility centre offers hope to families on the journey to parenthood. Led by experienced fertility specialists, we provide personalised, evidence-based care in a warm and supportive environment.

  • Fertility assessment for men and women
  • In-vitro fertilisation (IVF)
  • Intrauterine insemination (IUI)
  • Hormonal therapy and ovulation induction
  • Emotional and counselling support throughout

Maternity & Antenatal Care

Mother & Child

We walk with mothers from the very beginning — through pregnancy, delivery, and the precious early days of motherhood. Our maternity unit is warm, safe, and staffed by caring professionals.

  • Antenatal check-ups and monitoring
  • Safe delivery (normal and caesarean)
  • Postnatal care for mother and baby
  • Newborn screening and paediatric care
